# Community conduct

GlassHID welcomes contributors of every experience level. Be respectful,
specific, and patient. Discuss the work rather than the person, assume good
intent, and make room for people who are still learning.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate
disruption are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to keep the
project safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports should be
handled discreetly, promptly, and without retaliation.
